About

I’m a B.Sc. student in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at American International University-Bangladesh (2024–2028), focused on telecommunications, fiber optic transmission and IP networking. My CGPA is 3.58.

I enjoy the practical side of networks: measuring loss, tracing faults to their cause, and writing clear Reason for Outage reports. I’m disciplined, punctual and comfortable working in field conditions.
